# r/manim Community User Persona

## Demographics

- **Primary Audience**: STEM students, educators, hobbyist programmers, and researchers.
- **Age Range**: Predominantly late teens to mid-40s.
- **Common Backgrounds**:
  - High school and university students learning Python and math.
  - Educators (teachers, lecturers) using animations for classroom visuals.
  - Researchers using Manim for presentations and lectures.

> “I’m noob at programming… I want to make explanatory videos and science education videos.”  
> — A beginner user inspired by educational content

> “I wrote a script to demonstrate the surface area of a 3D shape for my students.”  
> — A high school math teacher

## Skill Level

- Majority are **beginners** to **intermediate** users.
  - Many are new to Python and animation.
  - Frequent questions on installation, syntax, and debugging.
- Small core of **advanced users** and contributors:
  - Share code, publish plugins, and support others.

> “You need to have some solid basic understanding of Python to understand what’s happening in Manim.”  
> — Experienced user advising beginners

## Motivations

- **Educational Content Creation**:
  - To explain math, science, and computer science concepts clearly.
  - Inspired by creators like 3Blue1Brown.

> “Videos like Grant Sanderson’s made me want to learn Manim.”

- **Teaching Enhancement**:
  - Lecturers and teachers animate lessons for their students.

> “Used Manim to animate musical intervals for a music theory lecture.”

- **Hobby & Skill Development**:
  - Fun, creative way to learn coding and visualization.
  - Personal projects and self-improvement.

- **Professional Presentation**:
  - For conference talks, YouTube videos, and lectures.

## Project Types

- **Mathematics Explainers**:
  - Calculus, algebra, geometry, proofs, number theory.

- **Physics & Science Demos**:
  - Kinematics, conceptual science visualizations.

- **CS & Algorithm Visualization**:
  - Sorting algorithms, time complexity, logic gates.

- **Tutorials & How-Tos**:
  - Manim installation, usage guides, beginner series.

- **Creative & Niche Topics**:
  - Music theory, dataviz, puzzles, and visual storytelling.

> “Kinematics Part 1 — made with Manim to teach motion physics.”

## Tools and Platforms

- **Programming Environment**:
  - Python, IDEs like VS Code and PyCharm.
  - Jupyter Notebooks or Google Colab for cloud use.

- **Typesetting**:
  - Heavy LaTeX usage for mathematical notation.

> “Having trouble rendering a LaTeX fraction with a dynamic variable.”

- **Video & Audio Workflow**:
  - FFmpeg for rendering.
  - Camtasia or other editors for syncing voiceovers.
  - Audacity for audio cleanup.
  - Blue Yeti mic for recording.

> “I don’t use any editing software… just FFmpeg and script timing.”

- **Plugins & Extensions**:
  - LilyPond (music notation), Manim Slides.
  - NumPy, NetworkX for math/graph extensions.

- **Learning Platforms**:
  - Manim official docs.
  - Manim Discord (active support).
  - YouTube tutorials shared within the subreddit.
